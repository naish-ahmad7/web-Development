About The Artist

Arijit Singh is a famous Indian singer known for his emotional and melodious voice in Bollywood songs. He was born on 25 April 1987 in West Bengal, India. He became popular after participating in the reality show Fame Gurukul in 2005. Although he did not win the show, his talent impressed many music directors. Later, he worked hard in the music industry and became one of Bollywood’s biggest singers.

Personal Life

He is known for being very humble and private despite being very famous. He lives a simple life and avoids too much media attention. He is married to Koel Roy.

Awards and Achievements

Arijit Singh has won many awards including:

  • Multiple Filmfare Awards
  • National Film Awards
  • Padma Shri (India’s civilian award) in 2025

He is also one of the most-streamed Indian artists on Spotify.
